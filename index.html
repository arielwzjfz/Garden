    How to get your follower list
    1. In Instagram, open Accounts Center → Your information and permissions → Download your information.
    2. Choose Some of your information → Followers and following, format JSON, date range All time.
    3. When the download is ready, unzip it and import connections/followers_and_following/followers_1.json.

    Import a fresh download whenever you like. New names become seeds and anyone who has unfollowed fades. A plain list of usernames works too. If the site is hosted with a followers.json beside it, the garden reads that file by itself once a day.
